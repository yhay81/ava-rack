# Decisions

## Search routes, not a product index

The first validation asks whether structured query composition is useful. Ava Rack therefore does not scrape or republish BOOTH product data.

## Local avatar rack

Up to five avatar names and search settings remain in the browser. This removes registration friction and keeps potentially identifying avatar preferences off the server.

## Visual work surface

The first screen is a hanger input, avatar rail, mannequin-like category shelf, and search tickets. No oversized marketing hero or experiment copy appears in the product.

## Purchase boundary

Every route ends on the official BOOTH search page. Compatibility, licence, price, and availability must be confirmed on each product page.
